unavailable, the browser download service was unavailable, and no local 5.x
distribution existed. The probe compiled the exact 5.6.0 SHACL Core engine,
parser, and report sources over the verified Jena 6.2.0 base libraries. It

classes whose Jena 5 ARQ APIs no longer exist in Jena 6. None participates in
the Atlas SHACL Core workload. Results are therefore an engine-code regression
check, not a substitute for an exact 5.6.0 distribution measurement.
