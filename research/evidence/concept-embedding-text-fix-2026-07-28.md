# Dropping boilerplate definitions from the concept embedding text — 2026-07-28

> **Status:** Incomplete working note; not evidence and not adopted by RefSpec.

A pre-publication experiment identified two compounding causes and ordered the
cheap one first: the dense channel embedded
`prefLabel + altLabels + definition`, every one of the fused registry's
513,236 definitions used a template, and 74% of the median embedding string
was therefore text shared with as many as 440,598 other concepts. The sparse
channel already excluded definitions for that reason. The proposed experiment
would close the inconsistency, re-embed the registry, and rerun the ablation.
