# Parent-level domain taxonomy over pooled source domain labels

Date: 2026-08-19
Status: **Proposal, unattested.** Nothing here is asserted into the subject ring.

## Provenance, stated up front

The taxonomy below was generated by a Gut-Instinct agent given ~433 domain-tier
labels inline, with source-set attribution stripped, across three runs. It was
asked to find a parent level "without forcing it," and to use SKOS/SKOS-XL only
where relevant.

Two things about that provenance matter and are recorded here so a later reader
does not have to re-derive them:

1. **Every citation the agent made to files in this repository was fabricated.**
   Across all three runs it cited `research/registry-sources.md` — including a
   specific row number and a claim that the row attributes LDA issue codes to
   `eionet.europa.eu`. That file does not exist in the tree or in git history,
   no `.md` file in the repo mentions eionet, and the agent's usage record shows
   **zero tool calls** in all three runs. Discard every repo-derived claim it
   made. The reasoning over the label strings is what survives.

2. **The labels it was given were incomplete, and that was our error.** The OMB
   slice was truncated to 12 of 98 labels by a `LIMIT 12` in the query that
   assembled the pool. The cut is alphabetical: functions and subfunctions both
   stop at "C". Roughly 86 labels are missing — everything from Defense through
   Veterans Benefits. The agent detected this from the paste alone in all three
   runs. OMB is the cleanest pure government-action carve in the pool and it is
   the scheme most under-sampled here.

Warrant: if adopted, this is an **atlas-minted adjudication (E4)** under the
REF-035 evidence doctrine — the weakest tier, because no publisher asserts these
parents. It is a navigational overlay, not a publisher claim.

## The premise that was wrong

The pool was assembled as "domain-tier labels," implying one grain. It is not.
Three schemes contributed two or three tiers each:

| Scheme | Tiers present in the pool |
|---|---|
| EuroVoc | 21 numbered domains **and** 127 microthesauri. `24 FINANCE` is the parent of `taxation`, `budget`, `insurance`, `monetary relations` — all four are in the pool as its peers. |
| GEMET | 4 supergroups, 33 groups, 40 themes. `NATURAL ENVIRONMENT, ANTHROPIC ENVIRONMENT` (supergroup) sits above `ENVIRONMENT (natural environment, anthropic environment)` (group); `environmental policy` is a theme. Same words, three altitudes. |
| OMB | 20 functions **and** 78 subfunctions. `AGRICULTURE` is the function; `Agricultural research and services` is its subfunction. |

Consequence: author a ceiling over the flattened pool and some parents will end
up siblings of concepts that are already their own children.

## Recommendation 1 — author one tier, inherit the second

Do not mint a synthetic tier 2. The pool already contains three publisher-
maintained second tiers that disagree about where the joints are; a fourth
opinion has to be reconciled with all three, and every mapping then has to answer
"which level does this join at?" That is ~245 undecidable calls with no evidence
to decide them.

Author the ceiling only. Below it sit the source schemes' own tier-2 labels,
each keeping its native scheme identity. Two levels, one authored.

## Recommendation 2 — `skos:broadMatch`, not `skos:broader`

`skos:broader` is for hierarchy *within* a concept scheme. Hanging twelve foreign
schemes' labels onto a ceiling we authored is a mapping across scheme boundaries,
and SKOS has dedicated properties for exactly that. Use `broader` and you assert
the source concept is part of our hierarchy, which it is not; any reasoner walking
`broader` transitively will emit conclusions about EuroVoc that EuroVoc never made.

`broadMatch` is also the property consistent with `docs/concept-identity.md`:
concept identity is minted per source scheme.

Note for the eventual spec text: **a shared parent asserts `broadMatch` on each
child and asserts nothing between the children.** Someone will read a shared
parent as an equivalence claim. Say so explicitly.

## Recommendation 3 — zero `exactMatch` across schemes

Not one pair. `exactMatch` is transitive, so a single wrong assertion contaminates
every chain it joins. The concrete failure:

    CRS `Health`  exactMatch  GAO `Health Care`
    GAO `Health Care`  exactMatch  Census `Health`
    ⟹ CRS `Health`  exactMatch  Census `Health`

That last line formally claims a policy area covering FDA food labelling is the
same concept as a payroll line that **excludes hospital staff** (Census carries a
separate `Hospitals` function). It is an accounting boundary wearing a concept's
clothes.

Use `closeMatch` sparingly, with a scope note on each, and the chain never closes.
Do not use `skos:related` here — every case that tempted it turned out to be
"these are on different axes," which is prose, not an edge. Skip SKOS-XL entirely;
the interesting problems are all about scope and plane, which live on the concept.

Admission test: a parent earns a slot if **at least 4 of the 8 independent
publishers carve at that boundary on their own.** Twelve was requested; the
material pays for twenty. Compressing to twelve would require merging Finance into
Public Finance (GAO, CRS, OMB and Census all refuse), Education into Science
(EuroVoc refuses at 32 vs 36), and Energy into Industry (EuroVoc refuses at 66 vs
68, GEMET refuses at group level).

### Why the two provisionals are weak

**Industry** passes 4/8 and **all four are European**. EuroVoc gives it a top
domain (`68 INDUSTRY`) with nine microthesauri; GEMET gives it a theme and a
group; LDA gives it sectoral codes. CRS, GAO, CFDA, Census and OMB have no
manufacturing concept at all — the US schemes route it through Commerce. That is
a real difference in how the two polities conceive the state's relationship to
manufacturing, not a clustering defect. US-facing consumers will find this parent
nearly empty; collapse it into Commerce if that is the audience.

**Foreign Relations** passes 3/8 and is the weakest entry. More than half of what
would populate it is not a subject: `Europe`, `America`, `Africa`,
`Asia and Oceania`, `overseas countries and territories` are places;
`United Nations`, `European organisations`, `world organisations`,
`non-governmental organisations` are an entity roster wearing a subject's clothes.
`Foreign Trade and International Finance` is Trade × international scope;
`international security` is Security × scope; `international law` is Law × scope.
Strip those and diplomacy-as-subject is real but thin. If one parent is cut, cut
this one.

## Local second tiers — two earn their keep, one instructively does not

Author a local tier 2 only where fan-out exceeds ~25 labels **and** three or more
independent publishers make the same subdivision.

**Environment** — four publishers independently split pollution from natural
systems from resource use: GEMET (`WASTES, POLLUTANTS, POLLUTION` vs the sphere
groups vs `RESOURCES`), CRS (`Environmental Protection` vs `Public Lands and
Natural Resources` vs `Water Resources Development`), LDA (`Environment/Superfund`,
`Waste` vs `Clean Air and Water` vs `Natural Resources`), Census (`Sewerage`,
`Solid Waste Management` vs `Natural Resources`).
→ *Pollution and Waste* / *Natural Systems and Biodiversity* / *Resource Use and Conservation*

**Agriculture** — four publishers split primary production from food product.
EuroVoc splits it hard enough to spend two top-level domains on it
(`56 AGRICULTURE, FORESTRY AND FISHERIES` and `60 AGRI-FOODSTUFFS`); LDA splits it
three ways (`Agriculture` / `Food Industry` / `Beverage Industry`); CFDA splits it
(`Agriculture` / `Food and Nutrition`); GEMET splits it (`agriculture`, `fishery`,
`forestry`, `animal husbandry` vs `food, drinking water`).
→ *Primary Production* / *Food, Beverages and Nutrition*

**Government fails, and that is the useful negative result.** Splitting democratic
process from public administration is intellectually obvious, but only EuroVoc does
it (`political framework`, `electoral procedure and voting`, `parliament` on one
side; `executive power and public service` on the other). CRS fuses them in one
label (`Government Operations and Politics`); GEMET fuses them
(`ADMINISTRATION, MANAGEMENT, POLICY, POLITICS, INSTITUTIONS, PLANNING`); GAO has
administration and no politics at all. One vote of eight. Author the split and you
must assign each fused label to one side, and you will be wrong half the time.

## What is deliberately unparented

~60 labels, roughly 13% of the pool. Five distinct reasons — do not lump them.
None of these should take a `broadMatch` into the ceiling.

**Structural facets** — `FUNCTIONAL TERMS`, `GENERAL TERMS`, `ACCESSORY LISTS`,
`TIME (chronology)`, `EFFECTS, IMPACTS`, `PRODUCTS, MATERIALS`, `RISKS, SAFETY`,
EuroVoc `business classification`, `legal form of organisations`. These describe
how a term behaves, not what it is about. `TIME (chronology)` is where GEMET keeps
century and season terms; there is no subject called Time. Model as
`skos:Collection` — it groups without asserting anything is narrower than anything.

**Environmental compartments** — `ATMOSPHERE (air, climate)`, `BIOSPHERE`,
`HYDROSPHERE`, `LITHOSPHERE`, `ANTHROPOSPHERE`, `LAND (landscape, geography)`,
`SPACE`. A where-in-the-earth-system axis. Shoving all five under Environment is
common and costs you the ability to express *agriculture ∩ hydrosphere* —
irrigation — which is the reason compartments exist. Keep orthogonal.

**GEMET's four supergroups** — `NATURAL ENVIRONMENT, ANTHROPIC ENVIRONMENT` /
`HUMAN ACTIVITIES AND PRODUCTS, EFFECTS ON THE ENVIRONMENT` /
`SOCIAL ASPECTS, ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY MEASURES` / `ACCESSORY LISTS`. Read what that
is: state, pressure, response, and a junk drawer. It is DPSIR with the serial
numbers filed off — a causal frame, not a subject list. It sits *above* the groups
being parented, so parenting it inverts the hierarchy.

**Geography and jurisdiction** — `72 GEOGRAPHY`, `Europe`, `America`, `Africa`,
`Asia and Oceania`, `regions of EU Member States`, `overseas countries and
territories`, `economic geography`, `political geography`, `District of Columbia`,
and the EU-institutional cluster: `10 EUROPEAN UNION`, `EU institutions and
European civil service`, `European Union law`, `European construction`,
`EU finance`. That last cluster is jurisdiction fused with subject —
`European Union law` is Law scoped to Brussels, `EU finance` is Public Finance
scoped to Brussels. Decomposing to subject × jurisdiction is the highest-value
normalisation available in this pool, and it must be decided **before** anything is
minted, because `Foreign Trade and International Finance`, `international
security` and `international law` are all the same trick.

**Populations, vehicles, and residue** — three piles, same disqualifier:
- *Who, not what*: `Veterans` (×2), `Native Americans`, `Indian/Native American
  Affairs`, `Families`, `Civil Rights and Liberties, Minority Issues`,
  `Small Business`. A veterans' housing program is Housing × Veterans. File it
  under a Veterans parent and you lose veterans' health, education and cemeteries.
  `Small Business` is a firm-size facet that modifies any sector.
- *Statutes and funding streams*: `Affordable Care Act`, `Recovery Act`,
  `Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (IIJA)`, `Opportunity Zone Benefits`.
  These have expiry dates. A subject taxonomy containing statutes rots on a
  schedule set by Congress.
- *Not concepts*: `Other`, `general`, `All other & unallocable`, `ALLOWANCES`,
  `Allowances [Assigned by OMB]`, `miscellaneous industries`,
  `Tariff (miscellaneous tariff bills)`, `Total - All Government Employment
  Functions`, `GAO Mission and Operations`. The `Total` row is a sum over its own
  siblings — admit it and every rollup double-counts. `GAO Mission and Operations`
  is about the publisher, not the world.

So the shape is **one subject axis plus four qualifier axes** (place, population,
instrument, compartment) and one causal model that does not belong in a taxonomy at
all. The qualifier axes are scheme-specific — only EuroVoc and GEMET expose them at
top level, which is why they look like intruders in a pooled list.

Model the qualifier axes as their own `skos:ConceptScheme`, not as Collections:
`Veterans` and `Europe` have real identity and participate in real assertions. They
are simply not subjects. A Collection understates them.

## False friends — candidate negative tests

Useful independent of whether the taxonomy ships. Each is a pair a string-matcher
will get wrong.

| Trap | Why |
|---|---|
| **`Human Capital` (GAO)** | Federal workforce management — agency hiring, pay systems, succession planning. Belongs under Government, next to OMB `Central personnel management` and EuroVoc `executive power and public service`. **Not** labour policy. The most expensive false friend in the pool. |
| **`water` ×4** | CRS `Water Resources Development` = Army Corps civil works (dams, locks, navigation) → Transport/Infrastructure. Census `Water Supply` = municipal utility payroll function → Housing/Community. GEMET `water` = natural medium → Environment. `HYDROSPHERE` = compartment → facet. Four parents, one word. |
| **`COMMERCE AND HOUSING CREDIT` (OMB)** | Mortgage credit, FHA and the GSEs — a *finance* function that has housing as collateral. OMB filed it next to Commerce for a reason. Do not `closeMatch` it to `Housing`. |
| **`Accounting` ×3** | LDA = regulation of the accounting profession (Commerce). EuroVoc `accounting` = enterprise bookkeeping under business organisation (Commerce). GAO `Auditing and Financial Management` = government audit and internal control (Public Finance). No match of any strength between the first and third. |
| **GAO's information trio** | `Information Management`, `Information Security`, `Information Technology` are federal IT governance — Government concepts in Information clothing. GEMET `information` = environmental awareness. EuroVoc `documentation` = library and documentary practice. |
| **`Animals` ×4** | CRS = wildlife, veterinary, welfare. LDA = welfare lobbying. GEMET `animal husbandry` = production. EuroVoc `animal product` = agricultural commodity. CRS/LDA is a genuine `closeMatch`; neither matches the others. |
| **`Space` ×3** | GAO = NASA and national security space programs (policy domain). GEMET `space` / `SPACE` = outer space as physical environment. `exactMatch` here pulls satellite procurement into an environmental thesaurus. |
| **`Economic Development` ×5** | GAO = place-based grant programs. EuroVoc `economic policy` = macro. OMB `Area and regional development` and CFDA `Regional Development` = place-based. LDA `Economics/Economic Development` fuses macro with regional in one code and straddles. |
| **`Utilities` (LDA) vs Census's four** | LDA = the regulated private utility sector. Census `Electric Power`/`Gas Supply`/`Water Supply`/`Sewerage` = government-owned enterprises. Same services, opposite side of the ownership line — and ownership is the entire point of the Census category. |
| **Homeland Security vs Emergency Management** | CRS `Emergency Management` = natural hazards and FEMA; LDA `Disaster Planning/Emergencies` and GEMET `disasters, accidents, risk` match that sense. GAO/LDA `Homeland Security` = counterterrorism and borders. US administrative reorganisation fused these in 2003; the vocabularies never did, and they are right not to. |
| **`Equal Opportunity` (GAO)** | Largely employment discrimination and contracting — Labour-adjacent. CRS `Civil Rights and Liberties` is constitutional. EuroVoc `rights and freedoms` is human-rights law. Three parents. |
| **`chemistry` ×3** | EuroVoc `chemistry` sits under `68 INDUSTRY` = the chemical sector; LDA `Chemicals/Chemical Industry` matches it. GEMET `CHEMISTRY, SUBSTANCES, PROCESSES` = substances as environmental agents; its neighbours are pollutants, not factories. |

**A true near-match, for contrast.** CRS `Congress` and EuroVoc `parliament` /
`parliamentary proceedings` are the same concept differently scoped — legislature-
as-subject. A legitimate `closeMatch`; close rather than exact only because CRS's
covers the institution's own administration while EuroVoc's is split across two
microthesauri. Worth carrying so reviewers can see what a real match looks like
beside the fakes.

**Undecidable, needs a written call.** `Postal` (LDA). USPS is a carriage network
and a communications service simultaneously. Pick a parent and record the reason.

## Related, already settled

GEMET's organization layer is collection-shaped, and this is already handled —
see `src/refspec/atlas/v3_registry_vocabularies.py:1653-1690`. GEMET asserts
membership with `skos:member`, translated to `atlas:hasSchemeMember` (same
direction, same publisher, recorded as a predicate translation rather than an

and dropped as carrying no additional information.

# Blind validation, 2026-08-19

Two independent agents were given the label pool with source attribution stripped,
the original task framing verbatim, and an explicit instruction to consult no files.
Neither could see the taxonomy above. Both were a different agent type than the one
that produced it. **Both made zero tool calls** — genuinely blind, unlike the
original run, which made zero tool calls while claiming to have read four files.

Pools were rebuilt from `output/atlas-3.1-parquet-search-view-2026-08-19`:

| | Pool A | Pool B |
|---|---|---|
| Occurrences / distinct | 241 / 220 | 319 |
| Tiers | top tier only, clean | deliberately mixed |
| OMB | 20 functions (truncation fixed) | all 98 |
| Schemes | CRS 32, GAO 30, EuroVoc domains 21, LDA 79, Census APES 33, grants.gov 26, OMB 20 | same + OMB's 78 subfunctions |

Cross-scheme repeats were preserved rather than deduplicated, because repetition is
the evidence the admission rule runs on. EuroVoc microthesauri and the GEMET
organization layer were absent from the 08-19 view; they are exactly the tier-2/3
contaminants this document says to remove, so the blind pools are tier-clean by
construction on that axis.

### `Industry` is refuted as a standalone parent

Both blind runs folded it into Business/Trade/Industry. This document predicted

### The mixed-tier critique is confirmed independently

Blind B, with no knowledge of it, reported: *"roughly seventy title-case labels are
OMB subfunctions of twenty ALL-CAPS labels sitting beside them as apparent peers.
Any clustering, co-occurrence, or frequency analysis over this list double-counts."*
It went further than this document's method and refused to count OMB's two levels as
two independent backers. It also noted that **casing is the only surviving marker of
the budget-function scheme** — a formatting accident doing provenance work, which
will not survive normalisation.

### Doctrine converged point for point

Both blind runs, independently and without shared context, reached: `broadMatch` not
`broader` across schemes (same `broaderTransitive` reasoning); **zero `exactMatch`
anywhere** (same transitive-contamination argument, same Health chain); no authored
second tier (same reason — cross-publisher vouching is exhausted below the parent
line); `skos:Collection` for statutes, residuals and facets; statutes are not
subjects (identical four labels); `Total - All Government Employment Functions` is a
sum; `GAO Mission and Operations` is publisher self-reference; `Human Capital` is
federal workforce management; `COMMERCE AND HOUSING CREDIT` is mortgage credit.
Both also reject `owl:sameAs` (merges provenance, not just identity) and the generic
`skos:mappingRelation` ("always usable, never informative").

## Findings that supersede this document

**1. A single merged scheme is malformed before a single edge is added.** SKOS
forbids two concepts in one scheme sharing a `prefLabel`. `Energy` occurs 6×,
`Education`, `Housing` and `Transportation` several times each. `skos:inScheme` per
publisher is therefore a formal requirement, not a modelling preference.

**2. `HEALTH` (OMB) excludes Medicare**, which is its own budget function. Merging it
with CRS/GAO `Health` moves roughly a trillion dollars silently. Add to the false-
friend set. Related, from the same run:
- `Atomic energy defense activities` is the nuclear **weapons** complex, budgeted
  under National Defense though run by DOE. String-match sends it to Energy.
- `Emergency energy preparedness` is the strategic petroleum reserve — an Energy
  concept wearing an emergency word.
- `Public Welfare` (Census) carries Medicaid vendor payments, so it is **not** a
  subset of any welfare concept that excludes health. Folding it with `Welfare` /
  `Social Welfare` moves tens of billions of health spending into social services
  with no trace in the graph.
- `Natural Resources` (Census) bundles agricultural extension and soil conservation,
  so it leans Agriculture, not Environment. Identical string, different parent.
- `Education - Higher Education Other` is **non-instructional university staff** (a
  payroll distinction); `Education - Other` is a **level** residual. Folding them
  destroys both.
- `Postal` (LDA) is postal policy → Communications. `Postal Service` (OMB) is the
  entity's budget line, which OMB files under `COMMERCE AND HOUSING CREDIT`. Two
  runs independently found that this budget function contains the Postal Service.

**3. Transport-by-mode is an attested local second tier — a third case this document
missed.** Three publishers split transport on the identical axis: OMB (`Ground` /
`Air` / `Water transportation`), Census (`Highways`, `Transit`, `Air Transporation`,
`Sea and Inland Port Facilities`), LDA (`Roads/Highway`,
`Aviation/Airlines/Airports`, `Railroads`, `Trucking/Shipping`,
`Marine/Maritime/Boating/Fisheries`). Authored by the sources, not by us. The same
test was applied to education-by-level and **failed** at two publishers, so
`Higher education` and `Elementary, secondary, and vocational education` map
straight to the Education parent.

**4. Instance-vs-class is a distinct failure from the facet problem.** `Medicare`,
`Social Security`, `Postal Service`, `Deposit insurance`, `Department of
Defense-Military` are instances, not classes. SKOS has no BTI/BTP/BTG, so
practitioners fake it with `broader`, and inference then asserts that every Medicare
line item is a health-policy item when many are payment mechanics.

**5. SKOS-XL is earned — reversing this document's "skip it entirely."** Both runs
converged on one narrow use: making assertions about the *string itself*.
`Financial Adminstration` and `Air Transporation` are the publishers' actual literal
forms and must survive as `skosxl:literalForm`, with corrections attached via
`skosxl:labelRelation`. Plain `skos:prefLabel` is a literal and cannot carry that
provenance. *"You do not silently repair someone else's vocabulary; you annotate
it."* Everywhere else SKOS-XL is cost without return.

**6. `skos:notation` must be separated from the label.** `52 ENVIRONMENT` is
notation `52` + label `Environment`. Glued, it sorts under "5" and fails every
string match against `Environment`.

**7. Staffing splits are a facet this document did not notice.** `Police Protection -
Persons with Power of Arrest` / `- Other`, `Fire Protection - Firefighters` /
`- Other`, `Education - ... Instructional` / `- Other`. The distinction is sworn vs
civilian and instructional vs support — a personnel classification orthogonal to
subject. Making them children implies the taxonomy narrows by employment category.

**8. Portmanteau labels take two `broadMatch` edges, not one forced parent.**
`COMMERCE AND HOUSING CREDIT`, `Arts, Culture, Religion`,
`32 EDUCATION AND COMMUNICATIONS`, `Labor Issues/Antitrust/Workplace`,
`Real Estate/Land Use/Conservation`, `Science, Technology, Communications` and
others have feet in two parents. Choosing one is a lossy edit of someone else's
vocabulary.

**9. Residuals split into two kinds.** Bare residuals (`Other`,
`All other & unallocable`) are unjoinable. Scheme-scoped residuals (`Other natural
resources`, `Education - Other`, `Police Protection - Other`) have a legitimate
`skos:broader` **inside their own scheme** but can never be mapped outward, because
"everything my publisher didn't itemize" has no stable extension elsewhere. Two
publishers' `Other` are not the same `Other`.

**10. Emergency Management should probably split from Defence.** Blind B carries
`Emergency Management and Civil Protection` as a parent separate from
`Defence, National Security and Intelligence`. That is more internally consistent
than parent #19 here, which fuses them while the false-friend table simultaneously
argues they must not be conflated.

## A fair criticism of the test design

Blind B: *"Stripping source attribution was a mistake, and it partly defeats the
stated purpose. You told me repetition is signal. It is signal about lexical
convention, not about extensional agreement — and only attribution can tell those
apart. Every publisher count is my judgment, not evidence."*

Correct, and worth recording. Blindness buys protection against contamination and
pays for it in exactly this coin. Both runs nonetheless recovered the scheme roster
from internal evidence — Blind A named the `Financial Adminstration` /
`Air Transporation` misspellings as the tell for Census — which is honest inference

# Four-rater vote, 2026-08-19

Two further blind assigners were run on the identical prompt, giving four
independent raters against the fixed 19-parent set. All four made zero tool calls.

| Consensus | Labels | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 4/4 unanimous | 208 | 94.5% |
| 3/4 majority | 9 | 4.1% |
| 2/2 split | 3 | 1.4% |

## The finding that matters: majority vote is wrong on the false friends

Every documented false friend went the **wrong way** by majority, 3-to-1:

| Label | Majority (3/4) | Dissent (1/4) | Correct, per scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Human Capital` | 8 Labour | 17 Government | **17** — federal workforce management, not labour-market policy |
| `COMMERCE AND HOUSING CREDIT` | 5 Commerce | 6 Finance | **6** — mortgage credit, FHA and the GSEs |
| `Economic Development` | 5 Commerce | 15 Community Dev | **15** — place-based grant programmes |
| `Opportunity Zone Benefits` | 7 Public Finance | NONE | **NONE** — named statutory tax incentive |

Three of four independent raters fell into each trap. **Consensus is not truth in
this corpus.** This is the empirical case for the rule both blind taxonomy runs
reached independently: `skos:scopeNote` is mandatory on every homograph, and the
scope note overrides the vote. A grouping pipeline that resolves ambiguity by
agreement will get precisely these labels wrong, and they are the expensive ones.

`10 EUROPEAN UNION` failed the same way in a different shape: it split 2–2 between
Foreign Relations and Government and **no rater chose NONE**, though it is a
jurisdiction and jurisdiction sits outside the subject axis. Forcing a single-parent
choice produced a wrong answer from all four. Facet labels must be removed from the
pool *before* assignment, not resolved during it.
